## Path and naming rules

- Source path of a row: `SRC/<slug>.svg` with `SRC = Assets/IdleFactory/Art/Development/F003/Source`. Import path of a row: `IMP/<slug>.png` with `IMP = Assets/IdleFactory/Art/Development/F003/Sprites`. The slug is the lowercase kebab-case form of the stable ID plus `-v01`, for example `F003-MACHINE-MINER` gives `Assets/IdleFactory/Art/Development/F003/Source/f003-machine-miner-v01.svg` and `Assets/IdleFactory/Art/Development/F003/Sprites/f003-machine-miner-v01.png`.
- Stable IDs are uppercase `F003-<FAMILY>-<PURPOSE>[-<STATE>]`. A stable ID marked `*` is also the `presentationKey` that content version 2 (T097) writes for a resource, machine or Item, character for character (`contracts/gameplay-content-v2.md`). The other IDs are referenced by presentation code only.
- Source and import files share the base name. Do not overwrite a reviewed output with materially different geometry: revise this brief and increment its version first. Do not rename an asset after references or GUIDs exist.
- Every `.svg` and `.png` below is under `Assets/` and needs its own committed Unity `.meta` file. New directories need Unity-generated `.meta` files. This brief does not claim that any listed file, directory, GUID or `.meta` file exists.

## Production contract

- **Pixel system**: top-down orthographic, 1 cell = 64x64 source pixels = 1 world unit at 64 PPU. A slot is 2x2 cells (128x128 px). Pixel sizes in the inventory are exact; do not crop transparent bounds, rescale, pad or pack an atlas for v0.1 unless W003 shows a need (T111 decides).
- **SVG source**: explicit pixel `viewBox`, transparent background, flat fills and strokes, integer coordinates snapped to the Art Bible's 2 px grid, and none of: embedded raster, linked file, external font, filter, mask, animation, script. Build every label (`RUN`, `WAIT`, `IN!`, `FULL`, `PAUS`, `OFF`, `L2` to `L5`, `I`, `II`, `III`) as vector paths, not live text.
- **Method `T1` and provenance `P1`** (unchanged from F001): repository-authored SVG 1.1, rasterized with Inkscape CLI `1.4.2` using

```text
inkscape <exact-source-path> --export-type=png --export-filename=<exact-import-path> --export-width=<listed-width> --export-height=<listed-height> --export-background-opacity=0
```

  The Asset Artist records the actual Inkscape build and platform. A different tool or version, or another generator (ComfyUI, Blender, image models, stock art), needs an explicit brief revision and independent Art Director approval, and is never substituted silently (ART-6). `P1` means repository-authored primitives with no external asset source; any language-model assistance is disclosed truthfully with the `T1` metadata, ownership is the repository owner (`fjure`), and no public license grant is claimed because the repository has no root license file. Source PNG output is a candidate until T109 passes.
- **Palette, badge inks, non-color cues, noise limits, precedence and readability rules**: exactly as in the Art Bible sections 3 to 9. A row that lists a fill uses that exact hex value.
- **UI icons** are one-color white glyphs (`#FFFFFF`) that presentation code tints with the Art Bible UI tokens. They carry meaning by shape. Disabled and focus looks are produced by tokens, not by extra sprites.
- **Ports and power**: every row has ports `N/A`. No mark, badge or symbol implies a logistics port, belt, cable, socket, or a powered or unpowered state.

## Slot composition rules

Numbers are slot-local source pixels of the 128x128 slot, origin at the bottom-left, y up. The Art Bible section 6 owns the precedence order.

| Layer | Asset | Placement | Sorting order (recommended) |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | One slot base: `F003-SLOT-EMPTY`, one `F003-SLOT-LOCKED-L<n>`, or `F003-SLOT-OCCUPIED-BASE` | anchor (0,0) | 10 |
| 2 | One `F003-MACHINE-*` body, occupied slots only | anchor (0,0) | 100 |
| 3 | `F003-SLOT-SELECTED-FRAME`, selected slot only | anchor (0,0) | 150 |
| 4 | At most one `F003-BADGE-*`, occupied slots only | center (64,112) | 300 |
| 5 | At most two markers: `F003-MARKER-BOOSTED` at (14,114) and `F003-MARKER-OVERDRIVE` at (114,114) | occupied slots only | 300 |

A locked or empty slot never shows a badge or a marker. The world uses no other overlay per slot. The presentation may enlarge a badge or marker to its minimum projected size (Art Bible section 8) and never shrinks it below its natural size at 64 pixels per cell.
