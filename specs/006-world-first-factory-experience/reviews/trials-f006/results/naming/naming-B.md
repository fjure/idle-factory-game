## Hard pairs and overall note

- State glyph meanings are guesses from shape: down-arrow = missing input, circle-minus = inactive, three bars = storage full, two vertical bars = paused, hourglass = waiting. Boosted = double up-chevron chip, Overdrive = lightning-bolt chip (guess). Shapes are separable on all four filters; arrow and bars badges share a hue on the colour filters but differ clearly in shape.
- Machine type: miner (striped block) vs assembler (round gear) always clear.
- Running assemblers: one per tabL sheet shows a larger bright disc (tabL gray 18, deut 13, prot 1, trit 14) versus the normal centre glow; named running (no badge) but flagged unsure; it may be an animation frame or another state.
- Locked digits readable on all sheets.
- P3: hub dot vs no dot is the main hard pair on every P3 sheet (dot barely visible at 1x; guess). Ghost preview vs shortage marker rest partly on elimination; machine card, upgrade marker and store full marker are clear. I could not zoom beyond the delivered resolution.
