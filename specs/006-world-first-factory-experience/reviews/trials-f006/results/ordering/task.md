# SC-006 ordering task (independent reviewer)

Two folders, `set1/` and `set2/`, each with 10 images `triptych_01.png` to `triptych_10.png`. Each image shows three panels of a factory, labelled A, B and C. Order the three panels from the least to the most developed factory; answer as three letters, e.g. `BAC`.

Decide from what you see in the images only. There is no answer key in this folder. Give one line per triptych: `set / number / order / certainty (sure or unsure) / deciding visual cues`. Then a short overall note: which triptychs were hardest and why.
